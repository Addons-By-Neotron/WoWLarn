# Credits

The pictures in `Media/` are rendered with Blender by the scripts in `tools/render/`. Most of what is in them is
modelled and shaded by those scripts; these are the outside models and textures they use. Most are CC0 (public
domain): no credit is required, but it is given here with thanks. The throne, the fountain and the shield are CC-BY 4.0, which requires it.
